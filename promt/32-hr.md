# 32 — `hr`

**Prereqs:** 07, 09, 26 (treasury), 15 (subjects).
**Tables:** `Staff`, `EmploymentContract`, `SalaryPayment`, `SalaryAdvance`,
`SalaryAdvanceRecovery`, `StaffAttendance`, `LeaveRequest`, `TeacherSubject`.
**Routes:** `/hr`, `/hr/staff`, `/hr/staff/[staffId]`, `/hr/attendance`,
`/hr/payroll`, `/hr/advances`, `/hr/paiements`, `/hr/leave`.

---

Eight tables. Moroccan payroll rules are in `SchoolSettings` (prompt 07) — use
them, do not hardcode rates.

**`Staff`** — the person as an employee, distinct from `User` (the login).
`schoolId` (Cascade), `userId? @unique` (→ `User`, SetNull, relation
`StaffAccount`), `code` (from `staffCodeFormat`), `firstName`, `lastName`,
`firstNameAr?`, `lastNameAr?`, `gender?`, `birthDate?`, `birthPlace?`,
`nationalId?` (CIN), `cnssNumber?`, `bankRib?`, `phone?`, `email?`, `address?`,
`jobRole` (`TEACHER | DIRECTOR | SECRETARY | CASHIER | DRIVER | ATTENDANT |
MAINTENANCE | OTHER`), `jobTitle?`, `maxWeeklyMinutes Int?`,
`status` (`ACTIVE | SUSPENDED | LEFT`), `hiredOn?`, `leftOn?`, `notes?`.
`@@unique([schoolId, code])`.

Not every staff member has a login (a driver may not), and not every user is
staff. Keep them separate and comment why.

**`EmploymentContract`** — `staffId` (Cascade), `kind` (`CDI | CDD | INTERIM |
INTERNSHIP | SERVICE`), `startsOn`, `endsOn?`, `trialEndsOn?`,
`baseSalaryCentimes Int`, `weeklyHours Int?`, `status` (`DRAFT | ACTIVE |
ENDED`), `notes?`, plus **`activeKey String? @unique`** = the staff id while
`ACTIVE`, null otherwise. **One active contract per employee, enforced by the
database** — the same partial-unique trick as `CashSession.openKey`.

**`SalaryPayment`** — one payslip. `staffId` (Cascade), `periodYear Int`,
`periodMonth Int`, `contractId?` (SetNull), and the breakdown, all `Int`
centimes: `baseCentimes`, `allowanceCentimes`, `overtimeCentimes`,
`bonusCentimes`, `absenceCentimes`, `advanceCentimes`, `socialCentimes`,
`taxCentimes`, `otherDeductionCentimes`, `deductionLabel?`, `netCentimes`;
`status` (`DRAFT | APPROVED | PAID | CANCELLED`), `paidOn?`,
**`cashOperationId? @unique`** (→ `CashOperation`, SetNull), `notes?`.
`@@unique([staffId, periodYear, periodMonth])` — one payslip per person per month.

**Every component is stored**, not recomputed on read: a payslip is a historical
document and must not change when a rate does. `netCentimes` is computed once, at
approval, from CNSS (`cnssRateBps`, capped at `cnssCeilingCentimes`), AMO
(`amoRateBps`), IR (`irRateBps`), absences (`payrollWorkingDays`) and advance
recoveries. Comment that.

**`SalaryAdvance`** — `staffId` (Cascade), `amountCentimes`,
`instalmentCount Int @default(1)`, `status` (`REQUESTED | APPROVED | REJECTED |
PAID | RECOVERED`), `requestedOn`, `reason?`, `approvedById?` (SetNull),
`approvedAt?`, `decisionNote?`, `paidOn?`, **`cashOperationId? @unique`**.

**`SalaryAdvanceRecovery`** — `advanceId` (Cascade), `salaryPaymentId` (Cascade),
`amountCentimes`. `@@unique([advanceId, salaryPaymentId])`. Σ recoveries ≤ the
advance; the advance flips to `RECOVERED` when they are equal. This is the join
that stops an advance being deducted twice.

**`StaffAttendance`** — `staffId` (Cascade), `date`, `status` (`PRESENT | ABSENT
| LATE | LEAVE | HOLIDAY | SICK`), `isJustified`, `minutesLate Int @default(0)`,
`notes?`, `recordedById` (**Restrict**). `@@unique([staffId, date])`.

**`LeaveRequest`** — `staffId` (Cascade), `kind` (`ANNUAL | SICK | MATERNITY |
UNPAID | EXCEPTIONAL`), `startsOn`, `endsOn`, `dayCount Int`, `reason?`,
`status` (`PENDING | APPROVED | REJECTED | CANCELLED`), `decidedById?` (SetNull),
`decidedAt?`, `decisionNote?`. Approving writes the `StaffAttendance` rows for
the period in the same transaction.

**`TeacherSubject`** — `schoolId`, `teacherId` (→ `User`, Cascade), `subjectId`
(Cascade), `preferenceRank Int`, `isActive`, `notes?`.
`@@unique([teacherId, subjectId])`. This is what `classes` (prompt 22) filters
the teacher picker by, and what the timetable uses to suggest substitutes.

**Money leaves through `treasury`, always.** Paying a salary or an advance calls
the disbursement service from prompt 26 and links the resulting operation. Never
insert a `CashOperation` from this module. Never mark a payslip `PAID` without one.

**Permissions:** `hr.view|manage|attendance|payroll|delete`, school-scoped.
`payroll` is separate — seeing the staff list and seeing everyone's salary are
different jobs. Salary figures must be absent from the DTO, not hidden in CSS,
for a user without it.
**Nav:** section `rh` — `/hr` (10), `/hr/staff` (20), `/hr/attendance` (30),
`/hr/payroll` (40), `/hr/advances` (45), `/hr/paiements` (47), `/hr/leave` (50).

**Screens:** staff list and record with tabs (identité, contrats, présence,
congés, paie); a monthly payroll run that generates draft payslips for every
active contract, shows the totals, and approves then pays them in a batch; the
advances register; the leave inbox.
